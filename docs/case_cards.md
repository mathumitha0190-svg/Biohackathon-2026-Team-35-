# Demo Case Cards

## Typical PCOS

Purpose: show the complete pathway when PCOS evidence is strong.

Profile:

- age 27
- BMI 29.7
- irregular cycles, 48-day cycle length
- weight gain, hair growth, skin darkening, acne
- BP and random glucose flags
- elevated AMH and increased follicle counts

Expected output:

- high PCOS triage risk
- ovulatory dysfunction supported
- clinical hyperandrogenism supported
- ovarian morphology / AMH supported
- metabolic risk present
- recommended next step: endocrine exclusions and metabolic screening

## Lean PCOS

Purpose: show that normal BMI should not dismiss the PCOS pathway.

Profile:

- age 24
- BMI 20.5
- irregular cycles, 42-day cycle length
- hair growth, hair loss, acne
- AMH and follicle count evidence present

Expected output:

- medium or high PCOS triage risk
- ovulatory dysfunction supported
- clinical hyperandrogenism supported
- lean PCOS alert
- recommended next step: continue guideline pathway despite normal BMI

## Endometriosis-Like

Purpose: show differential triage rather than forcing all symptoms into PCOS.

Profile:

- age 32
- BMI 23.8
- regular cycle pattern
- no androgen signs
- high chronic pelvic pain
- severe menstrual pain
- pain with intercourse
- bowel/urinary pain around periods
- heavy bleeding, bloating/nausea, difficulty conceiving

Expected output:

- lower PCOS triage risk
- weaker PCOS guideline evidence
- endometriosis-pattern red flags
- recommended next step: gynecology referral or endometriosis evaluation

## Incomplete Data

Purpose: show low-resource fallback and missing-evidence handling.

Profile:

- age 29
- BMI 25.7
- irregular cycles, 40-day cycle length
- weight gain
- no labs or ultrasound

Expected output:

- history-only model tier
- incomplete exclusions
- missing-test recommendations
- recommended next step: collect minimum useful labs before ultrasound-dependent decisions
